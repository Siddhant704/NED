Charter vs. Scheduled

Two ways to move cargo — pick what the shipment needs.

Not a hard sales claim — just what each option is actually built for.

Charter

Guaranteed capacity, custom routing and a fixed departure window — built for cargo that's time-critical, oversized or too irregular for scheduled space.

Scheduled Freight

Lower cost, shared capacity on fixed routes and frequencies — the right call whenever the shipment fits a standard schedule.
