Who We Are

An integrated logistics partner, built for heavy industry.

Nedlloyd Logistics is an emerging integrated logistics organization and a preferred partner to some of the world's largest companies in Energy, Steel, Power, Oil & Gas, Infrastructure, Automotive and Rail.

Company Overview

One partner, every mode — air, sea and land.

Nedlloyd Logistics was conceived to provide professional, integrated logistics management services — helping clients optimize the operating capabilities of their supply chain requirements across every major industrial vertical.

We operate as an asset-backed logistics organization, specializing in multi-modal transportation of oversized and heavy cargo alongside standard freight, customs and warehousing services. Our capabilities span order management, international freight handling, customs clearance, and end-to-end movement of heavy and over-dimensional cargo.

We maintain a pan-India presence with global connectivity through an international partner network, giving clients end-to-end cargo visibility throughout the logistics value chain — from booking to proof of delivery.

Vision & Mission

What drives how we operate.

Vision

To partner with our clients to innovate and continuously improve their business processes and technology solutions — leading to enhanced efficiency and timely completion of every project.

Mission

To manage our clients' supply chain and logistics needs in an innovative and cost-effective way, backed by full shipment visibility and accountable, role-based reporting.
