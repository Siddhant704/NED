Data & Access Governance

How shipment data is protected.

Every Customer Dashboard action is filtered by role — an Employee only sees assigned jobs, a Client only sees their own shipments, and a Client's Customer sees only what was booked on their behalf. Sessions are verified on every action, and passwords are never stored or logged in plaintext.

Client-provisioned Customer accounts remain visible to Nedlloyd Admin through an audit log, so access to sensitive documents like invoices and customs paperwork is never granted without oversight.
