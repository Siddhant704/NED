Privacy Policy

Draft — pending legal review

Nedlloyd Logistics India Pvt. Ltd. ("Nedlloyd", "we", "us") collects contact and shipment information solely to provide freight, customs, warehousing and related logistics services, and to communicate with you about your enquiries and shipments.

Information We Collect

Name, company, contact details, and shipment-related data submitted via enquiry forms, the Customer Dashboard, or provided in the course of service — including Job, Container and Invoice numbers, and documents such as invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, and proof of delivery.

How It's Used

To respond to enquiries, process shipments, share status updates through the Customer Dashboard, and meet our customs and regulatory documentation obligations.

Role-Based Access

Customer Dashboard data is filtered by role. Employees see only their assigned jobs; Clients see only their own shipments; a Client's Customer sees only shipments booked on their behalf. Every dashboard session is verified, and passwords are never stored or logged in plaintext.

Data Sharing

We share shipment data with customs authorities, carriers and logistics partners strictly as required to execute your shipment. We do not sell personal or shipment data to third parties.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting info@nedlloydgroup.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated review date.
