User Guide

Using the dashboard, step by step.

Everything a Client or Client's Customer needs to log in, track a shipment, and manage access.

01Open Website & Log In+

Go to nedlloydgroup.com and click the Client Login button. Enter the User ID and password provided by Nedlloyd, or by your service provider.

02View Your Dashboard+

After login, your dashboard shows every shipment: Job No, Origin, Destination, Mode, ETA and current live status.

03Track a Shipment+

Click a Job Number, or search by Job No / Container No / Invoice No, to open the full milestone timeline with dates and remarks.

04Download Documents+

Inside a job page, open the Documents tab to view or download every uploaded file — Invoice, Packing List, BL/AWB, BOE, POD.

05Manage Your Customers (Clients only)+

Clients get a User Management menu to create and reset login IDs for their own customers, who then see only their own shipments.

06Password Reset & Help+

Client's Customers contact their Client for a reset; Clients contact Nedlloyd Admin. Closed jobs remain viewable in your History.
